Project Overview & Objectives

Natural rubber (Hevea brasiliensis) is a crucial commercial crop in coastal and Western Ghats Karnataka. Fungal foliage epidemics cause severe defoliation, latex yield reduction of up to 45%, and premature tree mortality.

🔬 Edge ML Inference

Complete Offline Operation

Enables smallholders in remote hilly plantations with zero 4G/cellular coverage to detect foliar diseases in under 80 milliseconds.

📊 Dual-Stage Severity

OpenCV HSV Segmentation

Quantifies necrotic vs chlorotic tissue percentages across 4 HSV color channels, categorizing damage from Trace to Severe.

🗣️ Bilingual Guidance

Kannada & English Audio

Full UI and spoken text-to-speech support in Kannada and English, ensuring accessibility for non-English literate local farmers.

Target Rubber Foliar Pathogens

The system classifies rubber foliage into 5 distinct diagnostic categories:

1. Corynespora Leaf Fall (CLF)

Pathogen: Corynespora cassiicola

Characterized by distinctive circular or irregular necrotic spots with a dark brown halo, often with a "railway track" appearance along leaf veins.

2. Colletotrichum Leaf Disease

Pathogen: Colletotrichum gloeosporioides

Causes anthracnose lesions, tip burn, and distorted tender flush leaves during high-humidity monsoon spells.

3. Powdery Mildew / Oidium

Pathogen: Oidium heveae

Appears as white powdery fungal patches on young tender leaves during refoliation season (January to March), leading to crinkling and leaf drop.

4. Healthy Foliage

Hevea brasiliensis Control

Vigorous, dark-green trifoliate leaves with no active fungal sporulation, chlorosis, or necrotic spotting.
